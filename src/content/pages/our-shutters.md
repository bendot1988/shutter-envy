## Window Shutter Installation Styles

### Shaped Windows

Shutters can be manufactured into almost any shape, from Apex windows to Arches and even portholes. One of our expert team will template and measure your shutter perfectly, to give you the shaped shutter solution you require.

### Track Shutter Installations

Track Shutters have the ability to give you complete light and privacy control while also retaining totally function with our Free-Floating option. They are a perfect shading solution for large bifold door installations, and patio and french doors.

### Bay Window Shutters

Bay Window Shutters are one of our speciality products. With the ability to use precisely measured and manufactured custom angled bay post, we'll ensure a perfect fit every time. Bay Windows can be fitted with any of the available styles from Full Height to Cafe-Style and Tier-on-Tier. All Shapes of Bay including Box, Hockey Stick and Bow Window bays can be transformed.

### Tier-on-Tier Shutters

Tier-on-Tier Shutters offer you the same levels of privacy and light control as a Full Height Shutter, while giving you the added benefit of fully opening either section to enjoy a scenic view or allowing more daylight to flood into a beautiful room.

### Cafe-Style Shutters

Cafe-Style Shutter installation is for when you only require privacy and light control to either the bottom section of your window, or a given height. Allowing daylight to flood into your room from the opening above while maintaining a high level of privacy.

### Full Height Shutters

This type of Installation is the most common, covering the full height of your window opening. You can choose to have all of the louvres operate together, or in two separate sections, with our "split tilt" option.
