## Shutter Styles to Suit Every Home & Business

### Bay Window Shutters

Perfect bay window shutters make the most of the natural light and privacy. Window shutters maximise the space inside your room while adding a layer of insulation.

The size of a bay window allows for various shutter designs, from café-style shutters to full-height shutters.

- Add value to your home
- Reduce energy bills
- Privacy control
- Add insulation

![Bay window shutters for your home](/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/bay-window-shutters-for-your-home-1024x525.jpg)

### Full Height Shutters

Made-to-measure full height shutters are one of the most popular shutter styles we supply and install.

Full height shutters cover the entire window – but a well-placed mid-rail can be added to create a feature that will match your window style. As well as stunning good looks, hardwood shutters bring many advantages to your home.

- Reduce energy bills
- Outstanding light and privacy
- Easy to maintain and clean
- Added kerb appeal

![Full Height](/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Full-Height-scaled-1-1024x677.jpg)

### Tier-on-Tier Shutters

Tier on tier plantation shutters offer flexible control over privacy and light – with separate top and bottom sections these hardwood shutters can be adjusted or closed independently. If you want maximum light through the day and absolute privacy at night, a tier on tier shutter style could be perfect for your property.

- Maximise the light
- Privacy at eye level
- Added kerb appeal
- Great for Victorian window types

![Tier on Tier](/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Tier-on-Tier-1024x715.jpg)

### Cafe Style Shutters

Café style shutters fit the lower half of your window, allowing maximum light while completely controlling privacy.

- Perfect solution for light control
- Complete privacy for bay windows
- More space for window features
- Look great in kitchens and bathrooms

![Cafe Style Shutters](/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Cafe-Style-Shutters-scaled-1-1024x768.jpg)

### Solid Panel Shutters

Solid shutters offer a simple, traditional look – swapping the louvres for solid premium wood.

Since there are no slats, this style needs to be opened fully to let the light flood in – but offers absolute privacy and creates a snug atmosphere when closed.

- Ideal for period properties
- Premium hardwood panels
- Adds insulation
- Offers complete privacy
