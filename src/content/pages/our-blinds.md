## Roller Blinds

Our premium range of roller blinds helps to give your home a crisp and fresh finish. We have a wide range of beautiful fabric designs and colour pathways available. Add some sophistication to your roller by upgrading to one of our matching or contrasting fabric cassettes, which finish the blind beautifully. We have motorisation options available, from integral rechargeable, battery motors or hard-wired Somfy motors. All have the option to be voice activated and added to a home automation system.

## Venetian Blinds

Venetian Blinds are a stylish, simple way to customise your windows, with the natural stains and painted finishes available in our Real Wood ranges to the clean cut and finish of our Faux Wood alternatives. Venetian blinds are a great way to control both light and privacy, by simply tilting the slats to the desired angle. We have the option of a Privacy slat to close out the outside world when you wish, or the ability to add your own style, with either a matching or contrasting tape finish. Motorisation is available to venetian products, which can work with remote, phone app, or even voice activation for ultimate convenience and control.

## Honeycomb Blinds

Honeycomb blinds are available in a wide range of fabrics and colours. The unique design of our honeycomb blinds help to control light and privacy, and even the temperature of your room. Added benefits of honeycomb blinds are the availability of blackout options, with our SmartFrame option offering you near-perfect light-blocking characteristics. Honeycomb blinds allow you so many options in terms of operation, from a standard bottom-down to the versatile top-down, bottom-up for total control. Honeycomb blinds can be operated via motorisation including Remote, Hub and Voice activation.

## Duo Roller Blinds

Duo Rollers are a horizontally-striped, stylish fabric, which allows you to control light and privacy. As the double-layered fabrics move relative to each other, you can move from opaque to translucent to full visibility with ease. With a range of fabric colours and types, all available with matching cassettes and an easy wipe clean fabric, Duo Rollers add a contemporary finish to any room. These can be motorised with either remote functionality or connected via a home hub for app and voice control.

## Vertical Blinds

Vertical blinds are an elegant way to control light and privacy in your home. All of our vertical headrails are built using the Vogue headrail system, which is available in different colours, to match with your fabric or internal decor.
