## Bobcat

- Standard RAL colour: White (RAL 9010)
- Corrosion-resistant, powder-coated, extruded aluminium hardware, with stainless steel components
- Fully adjustable pitch settings from 15° to 66°
- Incorporating the ART 250 invisible arm tensioning system, providing greater strength and durability
- TUV quality marked, CE safety marked, complying with CEN standards
- Face-fix or top-fix installation options
- Mechanical gearbox or RTS Somfy Motor operation options
- Wind Class 2

## Puma

- Standard RAL colour: White (RAL 9010)
- Corrosion-resistant, powder-coated, extruded aluminium hardware, with stainless steel components
- Fully adjustable pitch settings from 15° to 75°
- 2 x 4mm tear resistant steel cables, guaranteeing at least 25,000 movements
- TUV quality marked, CE safety marked, complying with CEN standards
- Face-fix or top-fix installation options
- Mechanical gearbox or RTS Somfy Motor operation options
- Up to projection 2000mm, wind class 3; above 2000mm, wind class 2

## Tiger

- Standard RAL colour: Textured Anthracite (DB 703)
- Corrosion-resistant, powder-coated, extruded aluminium hardware, with stainless steel components
- Fully adjustable pitch settings from 6° to 60°
- Incorporating the SPLENDOR invisible arm tensioning system, providing greater strength and durability
- TUV quality marked, CE safety marked, complying with CEN standards
- Face-fix or top-fix installation options
- RTS Somfy Motor operation
- Wind Class 2
- Optional light can be installed in the arms, creating a lighter atmosphere beneath the awning
