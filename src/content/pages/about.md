### History

Having worked in the Blinds and Shutter industry for over 15 years, we decided it was time to give the local area something different. A real family run business with the best interests of the customer at the forefront of everything we do.

Highly skilled team of advisors, surveyors and fitting team, meaning you will always receive the best advice and superior finish you deserve.

We only source our products from the highest quality suppliers leading to a high end finish to every installation.

So whether you are looking to introduce some luxury into your home with our fantastic range of Shutters, or simply looking for a versatile solution for privacy or room shading with one of our custom made blinds.

Shutter Envy has a fully trained and highly skilled team of blind motorisation experts, for any kind of automation package you desire. Whether that is remote controlled roller blinds, two directional top down bottom up Honey comb pleated blinds or high end voice activated full house blinds installation, we can arrange a full service to delight you.
