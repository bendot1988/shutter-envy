  - question: "Are aluminium shutters the same as roller shutters?"
    answer: "No. Roller shutters are chunky external units that roll into a box above the window. Aluminium plantation shutters live inside the room and look almost identical to timber or faux wood plantation shutters. If you want the plantation look indoors, you want aluminium plantation shutters."

For dry bedrooms and north-facing living rooms, faux wood is usually the smarter buy. Most homes we survey end up with aluminium on two or three openings only."
