## Window shutters for Leicester homes

Leicester's housing is a real mix, and the right shutter depends on the window in front of you:

- **Victorian and Edwardian terraces** — common across LE2 around Clarendon Park, Stoneygate and Knighton. Splay bays often sit right on the pavement, so privacy on the front room matters as much as looks.
- **1930s semis** — box and bow bays, plus wider rear windows and French doors that suit full-height panels.
- **Newer builds and extensions on the city fringe** — larger glazing, patio doors and bifolds, where tracked or aluminium shutters are often the practical choice.

**Bay windows and street-facing windows** are the most common reason Leicester homeowners call us. Each bay is templated so the panels follow the angles of the frame, and louvres let you tilt for privacy from passers-by while still getting daylight in. See our [bay window shutter cost guide](/bay-window-shutters-cost-uk-2026-guide/) for how bays are priced.

## Shutter styles we fit in Leicester

- [Café-style shutters](/cafe-style-shutters/) — cover the lower half of the window for privacy on street-facing rooms, with the top left open to the sky.
- [Full-height and tier-on-tier shutters](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/) — full-height for a clean floor-to-frame look; tier-on-tier when you want to open the top and bottom panels separately.
- [Faux wood shutters](/faux-wood-shutters/) — moisture-resistant and good value, a strong fit for kitchens, bathrooms and most living rooms.
- [Aluminium plantation shutters](/aluminium-plantation-shutters/) — slim, strong and weatherproof, useful on patio doors and conservatories.
- [Arched window shutters](/arched-window-shutters/) — made for shaped and arched frames.
