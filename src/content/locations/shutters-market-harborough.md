## Bay window shutters in Market Harborough

Market Harborough has a high share of period bays — splay fronts on Victorian terraces, box bays on later semis, and the occasional bow. Off-the-shelf blinds rarely follow those angles cleanly. Made-to-measure shutters do.

On a typical Market Harborough bay we:

- measure each facet separately;
- use custom-angled bay posts so panels meet cleanly;
- recommend [café-style](/cafe-style-shutters/), full-height or tier-on-tier based on how overlooked the room is;
- quote **per panel**, not a single vague “bay price”.
