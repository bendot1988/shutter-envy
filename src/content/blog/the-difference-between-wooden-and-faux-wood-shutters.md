*Choosing the right shutter material can make all the difference. Whether you’re after timeless charm or practical durability, knowing the pros and cons of each option will help you make a confident decision. Here’s a clear, side-by-side look at how wooden and faux wood shutters compare — so you can choose the perfect fit for your home, lifestyle and budget.*

## What Are Wooden Shutters?

Wooden shutters are made from natural hardwoods like basswood or poplar — materials known for their strength, rich grain patterns, and lasting beauty. These shutters offer a warm, premium feel that’s hard to beat. Their authentic timber finish brings a sense of craftsmanship and character to any interior, making them a favourite for homeowners who value both aesthetics and quality.

Because wood can be stained or painted in a wide range of colours, wooden shutters are incredibly versatile — equally at home in a classic Victorian townhouse or a sleek modern flat. The unique grain patterns of real timber also mean no two shutters are ever exactly alike.

## What Are Faux Wood Shutters?

Faux wood shutters — also known as composite or PVC shutters — are engineered to look like real wood while offering enhanced durability. Made from advanced materials such as ABS, polymer or a blend of wood and synthetic components, they’re an excellent choice for high-humidity environments like kitchens, bathrooms, and utility rooms.

These shutters resist warping, cracking and moisture damage, making them ideal for busy homes where functionality matters just as much as style. They’re especially popular in households with children or pets thanks to their low-maintenance appeal.

## Appearance

- **Wood:** Nothing beats the beauty of natural timber grain. Wooden shutters add texture, depth, and warmth to your interiors, with finishes that age gracefully and enhance with time.

- **Faux Wood:** Designed to closely mimic real wood, faux wood shutters look strikingly similar — particularly when painted in white or neutral tones. Most people won’t spot the difference at first glance.

## Durability

- **Wood:** When properly cared for, wooden shutters can last for decades. However, they are more susceptible to humidity and should be avoided in steamy or damp environments.

- **Faux Wood:** Tough and hard-wearing, faux wood shutters are ideal for high-traffic or moisture-prone spaces. They won’t warp, peel or fade easily and require minimal upkeep.

## Cost

- **Wood:** As a natural material, wood tends to be more expensive — both for the raw product and the craftsmanship involved. But many homeowners view it as a long-term investment that adds style and value to their property.

- **Faux Wood:** A cost-effective alternative, faux wood shutters provide great value for money. You can still achieve the classic shutter look without stretching your budget, making them perfect for family homes.

## Where to Use Each Type

At Shutter Envy, we often recommend [wooden shutters](/our-shutters/) for living rooms, bedrooms and home offices — anywhere you want to highlight elegance, warmth, and a more traditional or luxurious aesthetic.

For kitchens, bathrooms, utility rooms, or any area with higher moisture levels, **faux wood is the practical hero**. You get the same stylish impact with added resistance to humidity and easier cleaning — no need to worry about long-term wear and tear.
