Blinds are a flexible and often budget-friendly solution for light control and privacy. Available in styles like roller blinds, vertical blinds, Venetian, honeycomb and duo rollers, they come in many fabrics, colours and finishes. ### What Are Window Shutters?

Shutters are a more permanent solution, adding architectural interest and increasing home value. Made of hardwood, MDF, or waterproof ABS, they are custom fitted to your windows and provide excellent thermal and acoustic insulation. Options include full-height, café-style, tier-on-tier, and shaped shutters—ideal for bay windows or unique spaces.

### Things to Consider Before You Choose

### Your Budget

Blinds are generally more affordable, which may be better if you have multiple rooms to cover at once. Shutters cost more but can last decades with minimal upkeep.

### Aesthetic Preference

Shutters offer a timeless, elegant finish that boosts kerb appeal. Blinds offer a more modern, minimalist touch and come in more fabrics and finishes.

### Room Usage

- **Bedrooms**: Blackout honeycomb blinds or full-height shutters are best for light control.

- **Bathrooms/Kitchens**: Waterproof shutters (Java ABS) or moisture-resistant blinds are ideal.

- **Living Areas**: Café-style or tier-on-tier shutters let in light while maintaining privacy. Venetian or duo rollers are also great here.
