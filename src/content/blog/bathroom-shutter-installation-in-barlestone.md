We recently completed a **bathroom shutter installation in Barlestone** for returning customers — a wet-room style bathroom with dark stone-effect tiles, brushed gold fittings and a window sitting directly above the bath.

## Why shutters for a bathroom?

Bathrooms need privacy without turning the room into a cave. Plantation shutters are an excellent choice here because you can **angle the louvres to let in daylight while keeping a clear line of sight blocked from outside**. Fabric blinds struggle in a steamy space; shutters wipe clean and stay looking fitted.

For this Barlestone project we fitted a **two-panel white [plantation shutter](/our-shutters/) set** neatly in the window recess above the tub. The louvres are shown slightly open — soft daylight in, privacy retained.
