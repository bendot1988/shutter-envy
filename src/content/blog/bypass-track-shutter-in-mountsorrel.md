We recently completed a bespoke bypass track shutter installation for a home in Mountsorrel, designed for a wide opening where smooth access and practical light control were both essential. This style of shutter works especially well for patio doors, bi-fold doors and larger glazed areas because the panels slide neatly behind one another rather than folding into the room.

For this project, the goal was to create a cleaner and more permanent finish than curtains or vertical blinds while still keeping the opening easy to use every day. Bypass track shutters offer privacy, glare reduction and excellent control over daylight, while maintaining a streamlined look that suits both modern and more traditional interiors.

They are a particularly strong option for homeowners who want shutter styling on doors or wider spans without compromising practicality.
