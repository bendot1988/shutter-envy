## Why Plantation Shutters? The Honest Answer

There are plenty of reasons people look into shutters, but the ones we hear most often from Loughborough customers come down to three things: light control, privacy, and adding lasting value to a home.

> “Unlike curtains, plantation shutters let you control exactly how much light enters a room without sacrificing privacy.”

Tilt the louvres one way and you’re flooding the room with natural light while keeping the street out of view. Tilt them the other way for blackout-level darkness at midday. It’s a level of precision that curtains and standard blinds simply can’t match.

## What Makes a Good Plantation Shutter? (And What to Watch Out For)

**Not all shutters are the same, and this is something we’re quite open about.**

The market has been flooded in recent years with cheap imported shutters that look fine in a showroom photograph but warp, discolour, or fall out of alignment within a year or two. If you’re going to have shutters in your Loughborough home for the next fifteen or twenty years — and most of our customers do — it’s worth knowing what to ask for.

Real wood shutters are beautiful and carry a natural warmth, but they can be sensitive to moisture — which makes them less ideal for bathrooms or kitchens where humidity fluctuates. For those rooms, we typically recommend faux wood (MDF core with a PVC coating), which performs identically in terms of appearance but handles moisture far better and comes at a slightly lower price point.

The other key thing to understand is that shutters need to be made to measure. Every window in every home is slightly different. We take precise measurements on our home visit and the shutters are manufactured to fit those exact dimensions. This is how you avoid the gaps, the uneven louvres, and the ‘almost right’ look that comes from off-the-shelf products.

## The Most Popular Styles We Fit in Loughborough Homes

![faux wood shutter louvre close up](https://shutter-envy.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/faux-wood-shutter-louvre-close-up-1024x571.jpeg "Plantation Shutters in Loughborough: Why Local Homeowners Are Choosing Shutter Envy 2")

**Full height shutters** are the most popular choice we fit across Loughborough and the surrounding area. They cover the entire window from top to bottom in one panel (or in a pair), giving a clean, classic look that works equally well in modern new builds and older period properties.

**Tier-on-tier shutters** are the go-to choice for rooms where privacy matters most but you still want daylight — typically front-facing rooms on busier streets. The upper and lower panels open independently, so you can have the top open for light while the bottom stays closed against the pavement view. For homes on the main roads through Loughborough or the estates closer to the M1, this style comes up again and again.

**Café style shutters** — which cover only the lower half of the window — are popular in kitchen and dining rooms. They let in light from the top while keeping ground-floor privacy intact. We also fit bay window shutters, which require careful measurement and custom-angle joining — something that separates experienced installers from the less experienced.
