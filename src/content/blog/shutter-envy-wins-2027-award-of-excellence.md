We're incredibly proud to announce that we've been named a winner at the **E2 Award of Excellence 2027**.

Moments like this don't come around every day. Winning is fantastic recognition of the hard work, creativity and dedication that go into everything we do, from the first home visit through to the final tidy-up after fitting.

## A thank you from all of us

A huge thank you to our amazing team, clients, partners and everyone who has supported us along the way. Whether you've welcomed us into your home for shutters, blinds or awnings, left a kind review, or simply cheered us on from the sidelines, you've played a part in this.

We're a family-run business based in Melton Mowbray, serving homes across Leicester, Loughborough and the wider Leicestershire area. Awards are lovely, of course. What means even more is knowing that the people we work with every day are proud of what we deliver.

## Recognised among brilliant company

We're honoured to be recognised alongside so many brilliant organisations and individuals in the industry. The E2 Award of Excellence celebrates the people and businesses raising the bar, and it feels special to stand among them.

This follows our recent run of industry recognition, including successive IE 100 Awards wins. Each one reminds us why we keep aiming higher: better craftsmanship, clearer advice, and a service experience that feels personal rather than rushed.

## Here's to what comes next

Celebrating this achievement feels great. We're equally excited about what comes next: more beautiful homes, more carefully fitted shutters and blinds, and the same honest, family-run approach our customers know us for.

If you've been thinking about transforming a room with made-to-measure shutters, blinds or awnings, we'd love to help. [Get in touch](/contact/) for a no-obligation home visit, and let's make your space something you're proud of too.

Cheers to our customers, our partners, and our team. Thank you.
