Plantation shutters look finished, give strong daytime privacy and let you tilt light through the day. Close the louvres at night and the room gets darker — but **not fully black**.

That is the gap Mark shows in this short bedroom demo: shut the louvres, then compare with **[BlindScreen®](/blindscreen/)** when you need true sleep darkness.

## The Short Answer

- **Closed louvres = room-darkening**, not cinema blackout.
- **Light still shows** between louvres and at the frame — that is how shutters are built.
- **[BlindScreen®](/blindscreen/)** uses cellular blackout fabric for near-total darkness (and can add a fly screen on bifolds).
- **Many bedrooms use both**: shutters for the day, BlindScreen® for sleep.
- We measure on site across [Leicester](/locations/shutters-in-leicester/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/) and Leicestershire.

## What closed louvres actually do

Tilted open, louvres give soft daylight and privacy. Closed flat, they cut most of the view and a large share of the light — which is why shutters feel so effective in living rooms and many bedrooms.

They are still a panel system with moving parts. Gaps remain so the louvres can tilt and the panels can open. For a deeper explanation, see [Are plantation shutters blackout?](/are-plantation-shutters-blackout-bedroom-shutter-guide/).

## What BlindScreen® adds

BlindScreen® is a slim, made-to-measure cassette with honeycomb blackout fabric. Pulled across, it covers the glass as a continuous surface rather than a stack of louvres — so edge bleed drops dramatically compared with shutters alone.

On bifolds and patio doors the same system can carry an **insect mesh** in a dual track — blackout when you need darkness, mesh when you want the doors open in summer. More on that in our [blackout blinds for bifold doors](/best-blackout-blind-for-bifold-doors-leicestershire/) guide and on the main [BlindScreen® page](/blindscreen/).

## When to choose which

| Need | Better starting point |
|---|---|
| Daytime privacy and a fitted look | Plantation shutters |
| Soft morning light control | Shutters (tilt the louvres) |
| Near-total darkness for sleep / shift work | BlindScreen® (often with shutters) |
| Bifold summer ventilation without flies | BlindScreen® with insect mesh |
| Street-facing living room style | Shutters ([living room guide](/living-room-shutters-uk-buyers-guide/)) |

4. Whether a bifold also needs the fly-screen track.
