When it comes to shutters, the choices are endless. Online retailers promise low prices and quick delivery, but is that really what you want for your home? At Shutter Envy, we believe there’s no substitute for local expertise, personal service, and professional installation.

Here’s why choosing a local business like us is the better choice.




![Shutter Envy Social Posting 2](/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Shutter-Envy-Social-Posting-2-1024x1024.jpg)




- ### 1. Made-to-Measure, Not Guesswork

Big online retailers often expect you to measure your windows yourself. A mistake of just a few millimetres can lead to shutters that don’t fit properly — and once they are made, they can’t be adjusted.  With Shutter Envy, you get a full professional survey. Our expert team measures every window precisely, even the tricky ones like bays or shaped windows, ensuring a perfect fit. No guesswork, no stress.
- ### 2. Professional Installation, Not DIY

Online retailers deliver boxes to your door and leave you to it. Installing shutters might seem simple, but getting them level, securely fitted, and properly aligned is a skill. Bad installation can damage both your shutters and your home.  Our fully trained installers take care of everything. We handle the heavy lifting, the drilling, the fine adjustments — and we tidy up after ourselves too. All you have to do is enjoy your new shutters.
- ### 3. Personal Service, Real People

When you buy from an online giant, you are just an order number. Problems? You’ll be stuck with call centres or automated emails.  With Shutter Envy, you speak to real people. We’re based locally in Loughborough, serving Leicester and the surrounding villages. We come to your home, listen to your needs, guide you through your options, and stay in touch every step of the way.
- ### 4. Quality Products, Not Cheap Shortcuts

Online prices look good, but they often mean cheaper materials and limited choices. Poor-quality MDF, weak frames, and fewer colour options are common.  At Shutter Envy, we only source from top-quality suppliers. Whether it’s durable hardwoods or water-resistant ABS for bathrooms, you can trust that your shutters will not only look beautiful but last for years.
- ### 5. Local Business, Local Care

We are a family-run business. Your home matters to us because you are part of our local community. When you choose Shutter Envy, you’re supporting local jobs and businesses — not shareholders in distant offices.  And if you ever need aftercare or advice? We’re just around the corner.
- ### 6. Peace of Mind

With Shutter Envy, your shutters are backed by expert installation and proper aftercare. If anything’s not right, we’re there to put it right. That’s something you won’t get from a box dropped at your door.





### Why Settle for Less?

Choosing shutters is a big decision. Make it the right one by trusting a local, experienced team who will make sure everything is perfect — from the first measurement to the final fitting.

Get in touch today for your free, no-obligation consultation.

Be the envy of your friends and neighbours — choose Shutter Envy.
