Bathrooms punish the wrong window dressing. Steam from daily showers, splash risk by the bath, condensation on cold glass, and a need for privacy without turning the room into a cave. **Bathroom shutters** can work brilliantly — but only with the right material and a layout measured in the room, not guessed from a brochure.

This guide covers what we recommend on surveys across [Leicester](/locations/shutters-in-leicester/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/) and Leicestershire: waterproof materials, full-height vs [café-style](/cafe-style-shutters/) choices, realistic 2026 costs, and when blinds are the better answer.

## The Short Answer

- **Yes — shutters belong in many bathrooms**, especially bath windows and ensuites on uPVC.
- **Waterproof ABS or humidity-rated faux wood** is the default — not MDF or standard timber.
- **Full-height** suits bath and ensuite windows; **café-height** suits basin-level privacy.
- **Louvres angled closed** give privacy with daylight — better than a flat roller blind in steam.
- **Expect high-two-hundreds to mid-hundreds fitted** for a typical single window.
- **We measure on site** — splash zone, extractor and reveal depth decide the spec.

## Why Homeowners Choose Shutters in the Bathroom

Fabric blinds absorb moisture, can go stiff or mouldy, and rarely look as neat in a tiled reveal. Frosted film gives privacy but removes control.

[Plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) earn their place because they:

- Wipe clean with a damp cloth — no fabric to launder
- Tilt louvres for privacy while keeping useful daylight
- Look fitted against white or anthracite uPVC common in Leicestershire new builds and refurbs
- Stay stable when the window is open for ventilation after a shower

## Materials That Survive Steam

| Material | Bathroom verdict | Best used |
|---|---|---|
| **Waterproof ABS (Java ABS)** | Best for heavy steam | Wet rooms, baths without strong extractors, shower-adjacent windows |
| **Faux wood / PVC composite (humidity-rated)** | Excellent default | Most ensuites and family bathrooms |
| **Aluminium plantation** | Premium niche | South-facing glass, high heat build-up ([aluminium guide](/are-aluminium-shutters-worth-it-uk-buyers-guide/)) |
| **Hardwood** | Avoid as a bathroom default | Dry cloakrooms only |
| **MDF** | Do not specify | Swells and fails in humidity — see our [MDF verdict](/are-mdf-shutters-any-good-honest-uk-verdict/) |

The older question “can shutters survive the bathroom?” comes down to material grade. Our earlier [bathroom moisture notes](/can-your-shutters-survive-the-bathroom/) still apply: if it is not rated for wet rooms, it does not belong above your bath.

## Full-Height vs Café-Style in a Bathroom

### Full-height (most bath windows)

Panels from sill to head. You tilt louvres for privacy while keeping the room bright — the layout we fitted in [Barlestone](/bathroom-shutter-installation-in-barlestone/) above the tub.

![White waterproof plantation shutters above a bath with louvres angled for privacy while allowing soft daylight through](/images/blog/bathroom-shutters-privacy-louvres.jpg)

### Café-height (basin and cloakroom windows)

Lower shutters only; the top glass stays clear. Useful when the window is at mirror height and you want a permanent view of the sky while blocking the sightline from outside.

![White café-height plantation shutters on the lower half of an ensuite window above a vanity basin for mirror-height privacy](/images/blog/bathroom-shutters-cafe-style-ensuite.jpg)

### Tier-on-tier

Independent upper and lower panels — occasional on tall ensuite casements. Compare layouts in our [full-height vs tier-on-tier guide](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/).

## What We Check on a Bathroom Survey

Mark (or another surveyor) measures with the bathroom running as you use it:

1. **Distance from bath, shower and basin** — splash and direct spray paths.
2. **Extractor fan performance** — weak ventilation increases condensation load on every surface.
3. **Reveal depth and tiling** — louvres must clear glass and handles ([uPVC fitting notes](/can-you-fit-shutters-on-upvc-windows-uk-guide/)).
4. **Obscured glass vs clear glass** — some homeowners keep frosted lower panes and add shutters for light control.
5. **Material grade** — ABS vs faux wood vs aluminium for sun and steam combined.
6. **Colour against tiles and sanitaryware** — silk white remains the default; grey tones suit anthracite suites.

## 2026 Cost Guide for Bathroom Openings

Prices vary by size, style and material. As a planning aid for Leicestershire homes:

## When Blinds May Be Better

We will say so if:

- The reveal is too shallow for louvres to open without hitting tiled returns
- You need true blackout in a bedroom ensuite where shutters alone will not darken enough — see our [bedroom blackout guide](/are-plantation-shutters-blackout-bedroom-shutter-guide/)
- A fully glazed shower enclosure has no suitable frame point for plantation panels
