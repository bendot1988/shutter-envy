We recently completed a **bedroom shutter installation in Leicester** — **Pure White** [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) on a three-panel window, specified with **76mm louvres**, **hidden tilt**, **split tilt** and **matching hinges**.

## The spec

**Hidden tilt** takes the rod off the face of the panel, so the shutters read as a clean set of louvres beside the bed. **Split tilt** lets the upper and lower louvres move independently: privacy at pillow height, daylight above. **76mm louvres** open the view more than a narrow blade without looking oversized on a window of this width. Our [louvre size guide](/shutter-louvre-sizes-uk-guide/) explains how we choose between sizes.

For sleep and light control more generally, see the [bedroom shutters guide](/bedroom-shutters-uk-buyers-guide/).
