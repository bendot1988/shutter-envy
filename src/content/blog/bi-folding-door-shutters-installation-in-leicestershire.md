These bi-folding door shutters were installed for a client in Leicestershire who wanted better privacy and light control without losing the clean, architectural look of the doors themselves. Door openings of this kind need a made-to-measure solution, and shutters can work extremely well when they are designed around access, clearance and everyday use.

For this project, the shutters were fitted to sit neatly across the glazed doors while still allowing the space to feel open, bright and practical. This gives the homeowner far more control over sunlight and overlooking than curtains alone, while also creating a more integrated, high-end finish.

Bi-fold door shutters are a strong choice for homes with garden rooms, kitchen extensions or large rear elevations where both function and appearance matter.
