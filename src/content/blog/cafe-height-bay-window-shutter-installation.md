A recent **bay window installation** featuring bespoke **café-height shutters**. The perfect balance of privacy, light and style — ideal for making the most of a beautiful bay window.

## Why café-height on a bay?

[Café-style shutters](/cafe-style-shutters/) cover the lower portion of the glass only. That keeps sofa-height views private while the top stays open for daylight — and on a period bay it leaves decorative upper glazing on show.

For this project we fitted white café-height panels across a five-sided bay with leaded lights in the upper panes. The shutters follow the angles of the bay so each leaf sits flush in its reveal.
