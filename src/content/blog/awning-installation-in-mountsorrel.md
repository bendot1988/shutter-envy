We recently completed a **made-to-measure awning installation in Mountsorrel**, Leicestershire — turning a sunny rear patio into a usable outdoor living space. The homeowners wanted shade over their French doors without losing the bright, open feel of the garden, and a retractable [awning](/awnings/) was the ideal solution.

## The brief

The property has a paved patio directly outside the kitchen and conservatory, with seating that was rarely used on hot afternoons because there was no cover from direct sun. Curtains or fixed canopies were ruled out early: the owners wanted something that could extend when needed and tuck away neatly when the weather was cooler.

Our survey measured the fixing width above the doors, checked the projection needed to cover the seating area, and talked through fabric options that would complement the red brick and the existing garden planting.

## The finished installation

The finished awning features **bold vertical stripes** in deep terracotta and cream — a confident choice that works beautifully against the brickwork and picks up the warm tones in the patio furniture.

Key details from this Mountsorrel project:

- **Retractable cassette awning** mounted to the wall above the French doors
- **Striped acrylic fabric** with UV protection for people and furniture below
- **Clean white hardware** that sits flush against the brick when retracted
- **Motorised operation** so the awning extends and retracts at the touch of a button
- **Full professional installation** by our in-house fitting team

With the awning extended, the patio feels like an extra room — ideal for morning coffee, summer lunches and relaxed evenings outdoors without glare through the glass doors.

## Why homeowners in Mountsorrel choose awnings

Mountsorrel sits between Loughborough and Leicester, with plenty of period homes, semis and newer builds that open onto gardens through patio doors or conservatories. [Awnings](/awnings/) suit this mix of properties because they:

- **Reduce heat indoors** by shading south- and west-facing glazing
- **Protect furniture and flooring** from UV fade
- **Create usable patio space** from spring through autumn
- **Retract when not needed**, unlike a permanent structure
