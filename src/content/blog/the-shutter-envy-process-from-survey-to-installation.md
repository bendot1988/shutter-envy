## Your local shutter and blind experts, covering Charnwood, Leicester and beyond

At Shutter Envy, we believe the journey to transforming your windows should be simple, stress-free, and transparent. Whether you’re after bespoke shutters for your bay window or fully motorised blinds for a smart home set-up, our process is designed to give you full peace of mind — from the first visit to the final installation.

At every step, you’ll be dealing with our friendly, local team. We don’t outsource. We don’t cut corners. Just top-quality products and service you can trust.

**Ready to get started?**  
Call us on 0116 210 6241 or fill in our quick online form to book your FREE home visit today. Whether you live in Quorn, Anstey, Barrow Upon Soar or anywhere in between — we’ll be there.
