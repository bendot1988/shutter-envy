## Why Motorised Blinds?

				

				

The biggest benefit of motorised blinds is hands-free control. You can:

				

				

- Raise blinds at sunrise automatically
- Lower them at sunset for privacy
- Adjust them on demand via voice or mobile app
- Create schedules based on room usage or lifestyle

				

				

This is ideal for rooms like lounges, home cinemas, and bedrooms — where convenience, privacy and lighting all matter.

Not only does this modern solution enhance comfort, but it also helps with energy efficiency. By lowering blinds during peak sunlight hours in summer or keeping the warmth in during winter nights, you reduce heating and cooling needs naturally.

## Battery Powered Convenience

				

				

One of the myths we often hear is that motorised blinds must be complicated or need rewiring. Not with Shutter Envy.

Many of our motorised blinds come with **integrated rechargeable battery motors** — no cables, no wall damage, no electrician needed. You simply plug them in when needed (think charging your phone), and you’re good for months.

Prefer a more permanent solution? We also offer hard-wired systems using trusted names like Somfy, all fully compatible with your smart home setup.
