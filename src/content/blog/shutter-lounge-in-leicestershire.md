We recently completed a **living-room shutter installation in Langham** — Pure White [Boston](/our-shutters/) shutters on a wide multi-panel casement, specified with **76mm louvres** and **hidden split tilts**.

## The brief

This front living-room window needed a fitted cover that still let daylight in without a visible tilt rod across every slat. Boston in Pure White was the right range: hardwood-panel construction with a clean face, and split tilt so the lower louvres can close for sofa-height privacy while the tops stay open for the street view.

For style choices in front rooms, see our [living room shutters UK buyer's guide](/living-room-shutters-uk-buyers-guide/).

## The install

Five white panels were fitted across the opening against soft blue walls. Louvres sit partly open here — soft daylight in, privacy retained at sitting height. Hidden tilt keeps the panel faces tidy; split tilt still gives independent upper and lower control without a full tier-on-tier layout.
