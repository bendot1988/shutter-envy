We recently completed a **shutter installation in Loughborough** for a returning customer — white [tier-on-tier plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) on a three-sided bay, set against deep plum walls and period moulding.

## The brief

Bay windows need panels that follow every angle of the reveal. This Loughborough home also needed flexible privacy on a garden-facing bay: keep the lower half covered at sofa height while opening the tops for trees and daylight. [Tier-on-tier](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/) was the right layout — separate upper and lower panels on each leaf of the bay.

## After

White tier-on-tier sets were fitted across the centre and side panes. Tops can fold open while the bottoms stay closed, or both tiers stay shut with louvres adjusted for soft light.
