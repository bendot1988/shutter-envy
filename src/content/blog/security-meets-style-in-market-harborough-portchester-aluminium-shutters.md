## What are Portchester Aluminium Shutters?

Portchester shutters are essentially ‘plantation shutters with a secret’. To the naked eye, they look just like our elegant hardwood ranges. However, they are crafted from **architectural-grade aluminium**. This makes them incredibly strong, lightweight, and entirely rust-resistant.

The standout feature is the **patented Italian locking system**. Unlike standard shutters, Portchester panels include a lock and key. When locked, the louvres can still be tilted to control light, but the panels themselves are secured into the frame, creating a formidable barrier against intruders.

## Why Choose Portchester for Your Home?

Many homeowners worry that adding security means sacrificing ‘kerb appeal’. Portchester shutters prove that you can have both. Here is why they are becoming a popular choice across **Leicestershire**:

- **Intruder Resistance:** They are designed as a genuine alternative to security grilles. The metal construction and tamper-proof locks provide peace of mind for vulnerable ground-floor windows.
- **Indoor and Outdoor Use:** Because they are made of powder-coated aluminium, they are unaffected by moisture. They work perfectly in bathrooms or even on external patios and balconies.
- **Versatile Light Control:** Featuring **89mm louvres** and a ‘silent tilt’ (no visible rod), you can adjust your privacy and light levels just like a standard shutter.
- **Style Match:** They can be finished in satin white, charcoal, light grey, or custom colours. This allows you to mix them with wooden shutters in other rooms while maintaining a consistent look throughout the house.
