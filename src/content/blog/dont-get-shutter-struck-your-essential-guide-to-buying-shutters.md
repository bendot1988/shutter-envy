### How to Choose the Right Shutters: **5 Red Flags You Can't Ignore**

				

				

## Red Flag #1: No In-Person Samples

				

				

**Imagine buying a car without a test drive**. Unthinkable, right? The same applies to [shutters](/the-complete-uk-guide-to-plantation-shutters/). If a company is unwilling to show you samples of their materials and finishes in person, that’s a major red flag.

You need to touch the wood, feel the weight, and see the colours in your own home’s lighting conditions. Photographs online simply don’t do justice to the quality and craftsmanship. At [Shutter Envy](/about/), we always bring a comprehensive selection of samples to your consultation, so you can truly appreciate the difference in quality and make an informed decision.

				

				
															![bold](/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/bold-1024x559.webp)															

				

					
## Red Flag #2: A Rushed Consultation

				

				

Buying shutters isn’t a five-minute job. It requires careful consideration, precise measurements, and expert advice tailored to your unique windows and preferences.

If a consultant rushes through your appointment, taking hurried measurements or offering generic suggestions, consider it a warning sign. A reputable company will take the time to understand your needs, discuss different styles, materials, and operational systems, and provide detailed recommendations. We pride ourselves on thorough, unhurried consultations, ensuring every detail is covered and every question answered. We’ll work with you to design shutters that not only look fantastic but also function perfectly for your home.

				

				

## Red Flag #3: A Quote That's Too Good to Be True

				

				

We all love a bargain, but when it comes to bespoke shutters, an unbelievably low price often indicates a compromise on quality. Cheap shutters usually mean cheap materials, shoddy craftsmanship, and a lack of durability. While the initial saving might seem appealing, you could end up with shutters that warp, fade, or break down prematurely, costing you more in the long run. Invest in quality, and your shutters will last for years, adding value and beauty to your home. We offer competitive pricing for premium products, ensuring you get excellent value without sacrificing quality.

				

				
															![P1050628](/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/P1050628.jpg)															

				

					
## Red Flog #4: Hidden Costs and Omitted Details

				

				

Surprise charges are never a pleasant experience. A trustworthy company will be upfront about all costs involved, including:

- Installation
- VAT
- Any other potential expenses.

If a quote seems vague or doesn’t explicitly mention the full, all-inclusive price, ask for clarification. Don’t be shy about inquiring about every aspect of the cost. We believe in complete transparency, providing detailed quotes that outline every single cost from start to finish. You’ll never encounter hidden fees with Shutter Envy.

				

				

## Red Flag #5: No Clean-Up After Fitting

				

				

This might seem minor, but it speaks volumes about a company’s professionalism and respect for your home. Fitting shutters involves tools, dust, and packaging. If the installers leave your home in a mess, it’s a massive red flag. **A truly professional team will treat your home with the utmost care**, cleaning up thoroughly after the installation is complete, leaving you with nothing but beautiful new shutters to enjoy. Our fitters take immense pride in their work, which includes leaving your home as tidy as they found it – if not tidier!

***P.S. Always ask your sales advisor: ‘Will your fitting team tidy up and remove all rubbish after the installation?’***
