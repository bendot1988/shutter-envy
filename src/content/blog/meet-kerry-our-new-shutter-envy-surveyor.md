We’re excited to welcome Kerry to the Shutter Envy family!

With over 8 years of experience in the blinds and shutters industry, Kerry brings a wealth of knowledge and a friendly face to our surveyor team. Kerry is already out and about across the area, helping customers discover just how great their windows can look. Book your free consultation today and see how Kerry and the team can help transform your space.
