## The short answer  {#short-answer}

For most Leicester homes, plantation shutters give more daytime privacy than
curtains — you can angle the slats so the room is visible from the inside
but not from the street.

## Daytime privacy  {#daytime}

Curtains are binary: open or closed. Shutters let you tilt slats to let
light in while blocking sightlines from outside.

## Maintenance  {#maintenance}

Shutters wipe clean. Curtains need taking down and washing.

## Verdict  {#verdict}

For street-facing rooms, shutters win. For pure decorative drama, curtains
still have a place — many of our customers fit both.
