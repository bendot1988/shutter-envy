We recently completed a **bay window shutter installation in Lowdham**, Nottinghamshire — **Silk White** [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) with **76mm louvres** and **silent tilt**, replacing Roman blinds on a box bay.

## Before

The bay was dressed with three fabric Roman blinds. They gave basic shading, but when raised they sat in folds across the top of each window, and when lowered they blocked the light completely.

![Box bay window in Lowdham before shutters — three blue Roman blinds over the windows](/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/lowdham-bay-window-before.jpg)

## After

Shutters were fitted to each section of the bay, inside the reveal. **Silent tilt** keeps the panel faces clean with no visible rod, and **76mm louvres** let the room move from fully private to bright and open with a simple tilt.

![Silk White plantation shutters with 76mm louvres fitted across a box bay window in Lowdham — after fitting](/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/lowdham-bay-shutters-after.jpg)

Key details from this job:

- **Silk White** plantation shutters on a **box bay**
- **76mm louvres** with **silent tilt**
- **Replaced Roman blinds** for a fitted finish
- **Measured, manufactured and fitted** by our own team
