We were invited back by a **returning customer in Market Harborough** to fit shutters to their **bedroom bay window** — [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) in **Sea Mist**, with **76mm louvres** and a **midrail**.

## The spec

A soft colour like Sea Mist suits a bedroom where bright white can feel stark, and it sits well against painted woodwork. The **midrail** is lined up with the window's transom, so the lower louvres can close for privacy while the upper louvres stay open for morning light.

**76mm louvres** give a clean, contemporary look on a bay without crowding the narrower side sections. See our [louvre size guide](/shutter-louvre-sizes-uk-guide/) and [colour swatches](/our-shutters/colour-swatches/) for the options.
