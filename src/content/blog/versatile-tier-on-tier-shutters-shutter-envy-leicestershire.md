This recent installation in Leicestershire highlights why tier-on-tier shutters remain one of the most practical options for traditional windows. Because the top and bottom panels can open independently, this style gives homeowners far more flexibility than full-height shutters when balancing privacy with natural light.

For this project, the shutters were chosen to suit the proportions and character of the window while also improving day-to-day usability. Opening the upper section allows more daylight into the room, while the lower section can remain closed to maintain privacy from the street or neighbouring properties.

Tier-on-tier shutters are a strong option for front-facing rooms, period homes and spaces where light control changes throughout the day. If you are comparing shutter styles, you can browse our [full shutter range](/our-shutters/) or [arrange a home visit](/contact/) with Shutter Envy.
