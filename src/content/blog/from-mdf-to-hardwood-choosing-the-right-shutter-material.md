When it comes to window shutters, material choice matters more than most people realise. Not only does it affect the overall look and feel, but also durability, insulation, and long-term performance. At Shutter Envy, we offer a wide selection of high-quality materials — from affordable MDF to premium hardwood options. Here’s how to choose the right one for your home.

				

				

## Why Material Choice Matters

				

				

Shutters aren’t just about looks — they’re a long-term investment. They add privacy, control light, improve insulation, and enhance kerb appeal. Choosing the right material ensures your shutters match the conditions of your room, your lifestyle, and your budget.

				

				

## Popular Shutter Materials Explained

				

				

We use five main types of shutter materials:

				

				

- Antigua (MDF)                        
                         A strong, entry-level shutter made from Medium Density Fibreboard with a polymer coating for extra durability.
- Bermuda (MDF/ABS)                        
                         Combines MDF frames with lightweight ABS louvres. Great for larger windows or where weight matters.
- Cuba (Hardwood/MDF)                        
                         Hardwood panels with MDF frames. Offers the strength of wood but keeps the cost manageable.
- Java (ABS Plastic)                        
                         100% waterproof. Ideal for wet areas like bathrooms, kitchens, and utility rooms.
- Fiji or Samoa (Hardwood)                        
                         Made from premium hardwood, these shutters offer the ultimate in style, colour range, and finish.

## Making the Right Choice for Each Room

				

				

- Living Rooms and Bedrooms                        
                         Antigua and Cuba are popular here. They’re cost-effective and give a smart, clean look.
- Bathrooms and Kitchens                        
                         Always go for Java. The waterproof material ensures no swelling or warping.
- Bay Windows and Large Spaces                        
                         Bermuda is ideal as it balances strength with lightweight function.
- Showpiece Areas                        
                         Want shutters that really impress? Fiji or Samoa hardwood options offer richness, texture, and custom colours for that high-end finish.

				

				

## What Else to Consider

				

				

- Budget: MDF-based options are great if you’re working to a tighter budget.
- Style and Colour: Hardwood shutters give you the most choice in finishes.
- Durability: ABS and Hardwood tend to last longest, especially in tough conditions.
- Maintenance: All our shutters are easy to clean, but ABS is particularly low-maintenance.
