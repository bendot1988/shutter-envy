Winter in Leicestershire can be beautiful – frosty mornings in Woodhouse Eaves, crisp walks along the Soar in Barrow upon Soar, twinkling lights in Quorn and Rothley – but it’s also cold, draughty, and expensive to heat. If you live in Mountsorrel, Syston, Birstall, Sileby, Anstey, Thrussington, East Leake or anywhere across Charnwood and the wider Leicester area, you’ll know how quickly heat disappears through old windows on a January night.

This is exactly why plantation shutters are one of the smartest home improvements you can make before the temperature drops.

## 1. Superior Insulation – Keep Every Degree of Heat Inside

Unlike curtains or standard blinds, full-height or tier-on-tier shutters create a solid barrier right at the window. The thick panels and precision-fit frames trap a layer of still air between the shutter and the glass, dramatically reducing heat loss.  

Independent tests show that well-fitted shutters can cut heat loss through windows by up to 50%. In real homes in Rothley, [Quorn](/locations/shutters-in-quorn/) and [Thurcaston](/locations/shutters-in-thurcaston/) we regularly hear customers say their living rooms feel noticeably warmer within days of installation – and their boilers aren’t working as hard.

## 2. Total Draught Protection

[Leicestershire](/window-shutters-leicestershire/) homes – especially the gorgeous Victorian and Edwardian properties in Stoneygate, Clarendon Park, and villages like [Swithland](/locations/shutters-in-swithland/) and Cropston – often have beautiful original sash windows… that let in icy blasts. Café-style or solid-panel shutters seal those gaps completely. No more rattling curtains or cold spots by the sofa.

## 3. Cosy, Scandi-Style Ambience

There’s something instantly comforting about shutters in winter. Tilt the louvres to soften the low winter sun streaming over the fields of Seagrave or Rearsby, or close them fully for that snug, cocooned feeling on dark evenings in Groby, Ratby or Glenfield.

Warm white, antique white, silk white or natural wood finishes all add a timeless, luxurious layer that makes any room feel more inviting – whether it’s a family kitchen in Syston or a bedroom overlooking the wolds in Burton on the Wolds.

With energy prices still high, every degree counts. Families tell us their heating bills drop noticeably after shutters are fitted – often enough to pay for the investment in just a few winters.
