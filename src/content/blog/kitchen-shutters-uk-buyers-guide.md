_Last reviewed: August 2026._

Kitchens are hard on window coverings. Steam from the kettle, splash risk by the sink, grease near the hob, and a window that usually faces a garden or a neighbour’s fence. **Kitchen shutters** still work well here — provided you pick the right material and a layout that clears the tap and window handles.

This guide covers what we recommend on surveys across [Leicester](/locations/shutters-in-leicester/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/) and Leicestershire: moisture-safe materials, café vs full-height choices, realistic 2026 costs, and when blinds are the better answer.

## The Short Answer

- **Yes — shutters belong in many kitchens**, especially sink and garden-facing casements.
- **[Faux wood](/faux-wood-shutters/) is the usual material** for steam and wipe-clean living.
- **[Café-style](/cafe-style-shutters/)** suits privacy at the sink with daylight above; **full-height** suits overlooked elevations that need more control.
- **Expect mid-hundreds fitted** for a typical single sink window — not thousands unless the opening is wide or part of a bay.
- **MDF and untreated timber** are the wrong default near moisture (see our [bathroom moisture guide](/can-your-shutters-survive-the-bathroom/) for the same logic).
- **We measure on site** — photos alone miss handle clash and reveal depth.

## Why Homeowners Choose Shutters in the Kitchen

Fabric blinds and Roman blinds look soft in a brochure and then collect cooking film. Rollers work, but they rarely look as finished as a recess-fitted panel set.

[Plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) earn their place because they:

- Wipe clean with a damp cloth
- Give precise louvre control for morning glare on worktops
- Look deliberate against uPVC or timber frames common in Leicestershire semis and new builds
- Stay put when the window is open for ventilation (unlike loose fabric)

## Materials That Cope with Steam

| Material | Kitchen verdict | Best used |
|---|---|---|
| **Faux wood / PVC composite** | Best default | Sink windows, utility kitchens, uPVC frames |
| **Waterproof ABS / wet-room grade** | Excellent where steam is heavy | Open-plan kitchen-bath zones, splash-prone reveals |
| **Hardwood** | Only in dry zones | Dining end of a large kitchen, away from hob spray |
| **MDF** | Avoid as a kitchen default | Dry lounge links — not primary sink openings |
| **Aluminium plantation** | Niche premium | Intense sun or high-traffic doors off the kitchen ([aluminium hub](/aluminium-plantation-shutters/)) |

## Café-Style vs Full-Height vs Tier-on-Tier

### Café-height (half height)

Ideal above a sink when you want neighbours out of view but still see the sky and garden. The upper glass stays clear; the lower shutters cover the sightline.

![White café-style plantation shutters on the lower half of a kitchen casement above the sink, upper glass left clear for daylight](/images/blog/kitchen-shutters-cafe-style-sink.jpg)

### Full-height

One continuous set of panels from sill to head. Better when the kitchen window is overlooked, faces a close fence, or you want to darken the room for TV zoning in an open-plan space.

![Four-panel white full-height plantation shutters on a wide modern kitchen window with louvres open for soft daylight](/images/blog/kitchen-shutters-full-height-style.jpg)

### Tier-on-tier

Independent upper and lower panels — a flexible mid-point between café and full-height. Useful on taller casements. Compare styles in our [full-height vs tier-on-tier guide](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/).

## What We Check on a Kitchen Survey

Mark (or another surveyor) measures with the kitchen as it is used day to day:

1. **Reveal depth** — enough room for louvres to open without hitting glass or handles ([uPVC fitting notes](/can-you-fit-shutters-on-upvc-windows-uk-guide/)).
2. **Tap and mixer clearance** — tall swan-neck taps often decide café vs full-height.
3. **Hob and extractor proximity** — splash and grease load on nearby panels.
4. **Window opening type** — casement, tilt-and-turn, or doors off the kitchen (French / bifold need different layouts — see our [French door](/plantation-shutters-for-french-doors-uk-buyers-guide/) and [motorised bifold blinds](/motorised-blinds-for-bifold-doors-uk-buyers-guide/) guides).
5. **Colour against cabinets and splashback** — silk white remains the default; soft greys and woodgrains are common with anthracite frames.

## When Blinds May Be Better

We will say so if:

- The reveal is too shallow for louvres to open properly
- You need true blackout for a kitchen diner used as a guest sleep space more than shutters alone can give
- A conservatory link or glazed roof needs specialist blinds rather than shutters (we may recommend Perfect Fit or other options instead)
