We recently completed a **Boston shutter installation in Loughborough** — Pure White [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) on a four-section bay, specified with **76mm louvres**, **hidden tilt** and **split tilt**.

## The brief

This Loughborough bay needed a fitted cover that followed every angle of the reveal, without a visible tilt rod across the slats. [Boston](/our-shutters/) (hardwood panels on an MDF frame) in Pure White was the right range; 76mm louvres keep the view open when tilted, and split tilt lets the lower slats close for privacy while the tops stay open for daylight.

## Before

The uPVC bay was left clear before fitting — four sections, including hopper lights in the outer panes.

![Loughborough bay window before shutters — white four-section uPVC bay looking onto the street](/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/loughborough-boston-shutters-before.jpg)

## After

Full-height Pure White panels were fitted across the bay. Hidden tilt keeps the face of each panel clean; split tilt still gives independent upper and lower louvre control without needing a separate tier-on-tier layout.

![Pure White Boston shutters fitted to a Loughborough bay — 76mm louvres open, hidden tilt](/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/loughborough-boston-shutters-after.jpg)
