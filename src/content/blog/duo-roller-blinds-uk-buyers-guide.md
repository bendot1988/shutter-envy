A duo roller blind is the made-to-measure option we specify when a room needs **daytime privacy and daylight at the same time**, without jumping to shutter panels. You may also hear them called **day and night blinds** or zebra blinds. Same product: two layers of striped fabric that slide against each other.

This guide is for homeowners in [Leicester](/locations/shutters-in-leicester/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/) and Leicestershire. It covers how the fabric works, where duo rollers earn their place, what they cost in 2026, and what we check before anything is ordered.

## The Short Answer

- **Duo rollers suit living rooms, kitchens, studies and many bedrooms** that want a softer fabric look than a Venetian.
- **Two striped layers** move relative to each other: opaque for privacy, sheer for filtered light, more offset for a clearer view.
- **They are not a blackout product.** Opaque bands still leave sheer stripes and edge light.
- **Matching cassettes** finish the headrail cleanly; wipe-clean fabrics suit kitchens and busy rooms.
- **Plan on about £180–£300 fitted** for a standard window. See the [blinds cost guide](/how-much-do-made-to-measure-blinds-cost-uk-2026/).
- **A front bay still looks better in shutters.** A duo roller is the blind to choose when you want striped day-and-night control on a mid budget.
- **We measure on site.** Fabric, cassette and motor are chosen for that opening.

## What a Duo Roller Blind Is

[Our blinds range](/our-blinds/) includes roller, Venetian, honeycomb, duo roller and vertical. A **duo roller** is the double-layer striped fabric blind. One roller system carries two lengths of fabric with alternating opaque and translucent bands.

Align the opaque stripes and the room feels more private while soft light still filters through the sheer bands. Offset them and more daylight comes in. Raise the whole stack when you want the sill clear. That is the whole idea — and why people search for “day and night blinds” when they want privacy without dropping the room into darkness.

## How the Layers Work

| Setting | What you see | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Opaque bands aligned | More privacy, soft filtered light | Daytime street-facing rooms |
| Layers offset | Brighter daylight, clearer view through sheer bands | Bright mornings and evenings |
| Blind raised | Glass clear, fabric stacked in the cassette | Cleaning, plants on the sill, wide views |

The fabric is typically wipe-clean, which suits kitchens and family rooms. A **matching or contrasting cassette** covers the roller and finishes the headrail so you are not looking at a bare tube. That cassette is part of the look, not an optional afterthought.

![Close-up of duo roller day-and-night fabric showing alternating opaque and sheer horizontal stripes on a double-layer blind](/images/blog/duo-roller-blinds-fabric-close-up.jpg)

Up close, the job of the product is obvious: solid bands for privacy, sheer bands for light. Moving the layers changes which bands sit in front of each other. It is not the same as tilting [Venetian slats](/venetian-blinds-uk-buyers-guide/), and it is not a honeycomb cell. Different mechanism, different look.

## Where Duo Rollers Earn Their Place

- **Street-facing living rooms**, when you want daylight and sofa-height privacy without closing curtains all day.
- **Modern kitchens and open-plan spaces**, especially with wipe-clean fabric and a neat cassette.
- **Home offices**, where glare on a screen needs softening without a full blackout drop.
- **Bedrooms that are not sleep-dark rooms**, when a soft fabric look matters more than pitch black.
- **A mid-budget alternative to shutters** on a rectangular window that is not a showpiece bay.

Wide bifold doors are a different job. The blind has to clear the doors when they fold. Start with our [motorised blinds for bifold doors](/motorised-blinds-for-bifold-doors-uk-buyers-guide/) or [BlindScreen®](/blindscreen/) rather than treating a window duo roller as the same product.

## What It Costs in 2026

For a standard window, a made-to-measure duo roller commonly sits around **£180–£300 fitted**. That is the planning range in our [blinds cost guide](/how-much-do-made-to-measure-blinds-cost-uk-2026/), which also prices rollers, Venetians, honeycomb and motorised rollers.

Three things move a duo roller above that band:

- **A wide or tall opening**, because of the extra fabric and roller capacity
- **Premium fabrics and contrasting cassettes**
- **Motorisation**, by remote, hub or voice — see [blind motorisation](/blind-motorisation/)

We quote after measuring. Two windows that look the same from the kerb often differ once the reveal, the handles and the fabric weight are on the survey sheet.

## What We Check on a Survey

- **Reveal depth and shape**, so the cassette sits properly without fouling the glass
- **Which way the window opens**, so the fabric clears the handle and the sash or casement
- **How much daytime privacy you actually need**, street-facing versus garden-facing
- **Whether wipe-clean fabric matters**, kitchens and utility rooms especially
- **Cassette colour**, matching or contrasting, next to the actual fabric sample
- **Who can reach it**, which is what pushes a tall or awkward window towards motorisation
- **Children in the room**, because a dangling cord is the wrong default — see our [child-safe guide](/child-safe-blinds-and-shutters-uk-guide/)
