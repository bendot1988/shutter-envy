We recently completed a **shaped shutter installation in Syston**, in the [Charnwood](/locations/shutters-in-charnwood/) area — a bright loft room with an arched window on one wall and a recessed dormer with a timber window seat on the other.

Off-the-shelf blinds struggle with that mix of shapes. Made-to-measure [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) follow each opening so the room keeps its character and you still get privacy and daylight control.

## Why shaped shutters?

Arched and dormer windows are common in loft conversions and period stock across Charnwood villages. Curtains hide the curve; a single rectangular blind leaves awkward gaps. **Shaped shutters** are templated to the glass, so the arch stays visible and the dormer panels sit cleanly in the recess.

For the commercial overview — templating, styles and costs — see our [arched window shutters](/arched-window-shutters/) hub.

## The Syston install

### Arched window + dormer

Two white shutter sets in the same room:

- **Arched casement** — panels cut to follow the curve at the head
- **Dormer window** — three panels over a built-in timber seat, louvres partly open for garden light
