### Why Choose Custom Shutters?

**1. Enhanced Kerb Appeal**

Shutters instantly add a touch of elegance to your home’s façade. Unlike curtains or blinds, they provide a **sleek, polished look** from the outside. Whether you opt for **full-height, café-style, or tier-on-tier shutters**, they transform windows into stunning focal points.

**2. Perfect Fit for Every Window**

Unlike off-the-shelf solutions, custom shutters are **measured precisely** to fit your windows. From **bay windows** to **arched and shaped windows**, our expert team ensures a **seamless fit** that enhances your home’s character.

**3. Light &amp; Privacy Control**

Custom shutters give you **complete control over light and privacy**. With adjustable **louvres**, you can let in natural light while maintaining privacy. Whether you need **blackout shutters for bedrooms** or **café-style shutters for living spaces**, we have a solution.

**4. Energy Efficiency &amp; Insulation**

Shutters act as an **extra layer of insulation**, helping to maintain indoor temperatures. They **keep your home cool in summer** by blocking out direct sunlight and **retain warmth in winter**, reducing energy costs.

**5. Low Maintenance &amp; Durability**

Unlike curtains that need frequent washing, shutters are **easy to clean** and **highly durable**. Our shutters are made from **high-quality materials** designed to last for years with minimal upkeep.

### Our Custom Shutter Range

At **Shutter Envy**, we offer a variety of shutter styles to suit every home and preference.

**1. Full-Height Shutters**

Covering the entire window, these are perfect for **maximum privacy and insulation**. They are ideal for **bedrooms, living rooms, and home offices**.

**2. Bay Window Shutters**

A speciality at Shutter Envy, our **precisely measured bay window shutters** fit all types of bay windows, including **box, bow, and angled bays**.

**3. Tier-on-Tier Shutters**

These provide **ultimate flexibility**, allowing you to **open the top and bottom sections independently**. Perfect for **balancing privacy with natural light**.

**4. Café-Style Shutters**

Covering only the **lower portion** of windows, café-style shutters are perfect for **street-facing properties**, allowing light in while keeping prying eyes out.

**5. Track Shutters**

Ideal for **bifold doors, patio doors, and large windows**, these shutters slide effortlessly, providing **full access** when needed.

**6. Shaped Window Shutters**

Got an **arched, porthole, or triangular window**? Our custom shutters are crafted to **fit even the most unique window shapes**.
