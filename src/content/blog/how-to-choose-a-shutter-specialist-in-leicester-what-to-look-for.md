### Check for Authentic Local Reviews

In the digital age, a company’s reputation is visible for all to see. You should look for a specialist with a high volume of positive feedback from customers in your specific area. We use Google reviews to provide transparency, allowing potential clients to see exactly what their neighbours think of our work.

A quick look at our recent feedback reveals the level of service you should expect from any reputable Leicester installer:

- Professionalism and Cleanliness: Sharon Brown noted that our team was ‘brilliant so professional not one bit of mess left’. A high-quality installer should always treat your home with respect, removing all rubbish and leaving the space ready to enjoy.
- Expert Advice: Sometimes, what you think you want isn’t the best technical fit. Amie Harrison mentioned she was ‘advised not to’ have shutters in a conservatory because they wouldn’t fit properly. A good specialist will prioritise the integrity of the installation over a quick sale.

### The Importance of the Survey and Installation Process

A specialist is only as good as their process. You should look for a company that offers a clear, four-step journey:

1. Home Consultation: This should be convenient for you and entirely pressure-free.
2. Detailed Survey: A specialist must conduct a full technical survey to find the best solution for your specific window type, whether it is a bay window or bi-folding doors.
3. Transparent Ordering: You should be kept updated on the progress of your order at every stage.
4. Skilled Installation: The fitting team should be highly skilled and employed by the company, not sub-contracted out to strangers.
