We are proud to announce that **Shutter Envy is now a BBSA Accredited Specialist** — formally recognised by the [British Blind and Shutter Association](https://www.bbsa.org.uk/) (BBSA), the UK's trade body for blinds, shutters and shading products.

For our customers across Leicester, Loughborough and Leicestershire, this is independent confirmation that we work to the standards the industry itself sets for product knowledge, installation quality and customer care.

## What is BBSA accreditation?

The BBSA has represented the UK shading industry since **1919**. Its **Accredited Specialist** scheme is awarded to companies that demonstrate:

- Sound technical knowledge of blinds, shutters and shading systems
- Responsible sales and survey practices
- Professional installation and aftercare
- Commitment to the BBSA Code of Practice

In short, it is a mark you can trust when choosing who supplies and fits window coverings in your home.

## Make It Safe — child safety matters to us

BBSA membership also underlines our commitment to **child safety**. We follow the BBSA's **Make It Safe** campaign, which promotes cordless and safe-by-design window coverings and responsible advice around looped cords and chains.

Every home is different — especially where children or pets are present — and we take the time to recommend solutions that look beautiful **and** meet current safety guidance. That might mean motorised [blinds](/our-blinds/), wand-operated options, or shutters with no loose operating cords at all.
