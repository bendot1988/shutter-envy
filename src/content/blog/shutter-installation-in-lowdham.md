We recently completed a **made-to-measure shutter installation in Lowdham**, Nottinghamshire — a bright bedroom where a roller blind was doing a job, but not as neatly as the homeowners wanted.

## The brief

The room has a three-pane casement looking onto the garden, sage-green walls and a radiator under the sill. The existing white roller blind gave basic shading, but it left the recess looking unfinished and offered limited control — either mostly up or mostly down, with little in between for daytime privacy.

The homeowners wanted a fitted look that still let the garden light in, with the option to close the lower half for privacy while keeping the top open. Full-height [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) with independent upper and lower louvre control were the right answer.

## Before

![Bedroom window in Lowdham before shutters — white roller blind partly lowered over a three-pane casement](/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/shutter-installation-lowdham-before.jpg)

## After

Three white shutter panels were fitted neatly inside the recess, aligned with the window frames. The louvres can be tilted independently above and below the mid-rail — so the garden stays visible at the top while the lower section closes for privacy.
