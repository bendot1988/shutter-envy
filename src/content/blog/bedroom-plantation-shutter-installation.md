We recently completed a **made-to-measure bedroom shutter installation** — a dark-green bedroom where a roller blind was doing a job, but not as neatly as the homeowners wanted.

## The brief

The room has a wide casement looking onto neighbouring houses and trees, deep forest-green walls and a radiator under the sill. The existing white roller blind gave basic shading, but it left the recess looking unfinished and offered limited control — either mostly up or mostly down, with little in between for daytime privacy.

The homeowners wanted a fitted look that still let the garden light in, with the option to close the lower half for privacy while keeping the top open. Full-height [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) with independent upper and lower louvre control were the right answer.

## Before

![Bedroom window before shutters — white roller blind over an open casement, deep green walls](/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bedroom-shutter-installation-before.jpg)

## After

Three white shutter panels were fitted neatly inside the recess, aligned with the window frames. The louvres can be tilted independently above and below the mid-rail — so the view stays open at the top while the lower section closes for privacy.

![White plantation shutters installed on a bedroom casement — after fitting, louvres open](/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bedroom-shutter-installation-after.jpg)

![Close view of the bedroom shutter install — top louvres open, lower louvres closed for privacy](/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bedroom-shutter-installation-detail.jpg)

## Why homeowners choose bedroom shutters

Bedrooms need privacy without blacking out the view. [Plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) suit that brief because they:

- **Look fitted**, not temporary like a roller blind
- **Control light and privacy** without fabric that collects dust
- **Work on uPVC and timber casements** when measured properly
- **Last for years** with a wipe-clean finish
