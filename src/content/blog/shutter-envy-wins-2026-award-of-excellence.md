Shutter Envy has received the 2026 ‘Award of Excellence’, marking the **fourth year in a row** that our family business has been recognised for outstanding quality and service. This award places us amongst a very small group of companies who have achieved consecutive wins, showing the consistency and high standards our customers already know us for.

The award celebrates businesses that offer something genuinely different in their marketplace, with a strong focus on quality and customer care. We’re proud that our team has been recognised for that. From the first home consultation to the final installation, every member of our team shares the same commitment to doing things the right way.

We’re delighted that the award judges highlighted our ethical approach, our attention to detail and the strong reputation we’ve built within Leicestershire and the surrounding areas. As demand continues to grow, our focus remains the same: to deliver the best service and an experience that makes your home the envy of your friends and neighbours.

To all our customers (past, present and future) thank you for helping us reach this milestone. We look forward to bringing more homes to life with our award-winning shutters, blinds and motorised solutions.
