**In a nutshell**: Full height shutters consist of a single panel that covers the entire window from top to bottom for a clean, uniform look. In contrast, tier-on-tier shutters feature two independent sets of panels stacked on top of each other, allowing you to open the top half for light while keeping the bottom half closed for privacy.								

				

									
At Shutter Envy, we understand that selecting the right window dressing is about more than just aesthetics; it is about finding a balance between privacy, light, and luxury. For homeowners in [Charnwood](/locations/shutters-in-charnwood/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/), and Leicester, two of the most popular choices are ‘Full Height’ and ‘[Tier-on-Tier](/british-made-shutters/)‘ shutters. While both offer the high-end finish our family business is known for, they function differently to suit various lifestyle needs.

				

				

## What are Full Height Shutters?

				

				

Full height shutters are our most popular installation. As the name suggests, the panels cover the entire height of the window from top to bottom. These shutters provide a clean, sleek look that complements modern and traditional homes alike.

### Key Features:

- Simple Operation: The panels open as a single unit.
- Privacy: Complete coverage ensures your home remains a private sanctuary.
- Insulation: Covering the full glass area helps with noise reduction and heat retention.

For those who want a simple yet elegant ‘transformation’, full height shutters are often the first choice. They can also be fitted with a ‘divider rail’, allowing you to tilt the top louvres independently from the bottom ones.

### What are Tier-on-Tier Shutters?

Tier-on-tier shutters consist of two separate sets of panels, one stacked on top of the other. They function much like a stable door. You can open the top panels wide to let in maximum light while keeping the bottom panels closed for privacy.

<b data-path-to-node="11" data-index-in-node="0">The Benefit of Superior Light Control:</b> The primary advantage of the tier-on-tier style is its versatility. Because the top and bottom halves operate independently, you have more control over the ambience of your room than with any other shutter style.

- Maximum Sunlight: Open the top tier fully to invite daylight into the room.
- Street-Level Privacy: Keep the bottom tier closed to prevent passers-by from looking in.
- Flexible Shading: Angle the louvres on both levels to deflect glare while maintaining a bright atmosphere.

### Comparing the ‘Look’ for Victorian Windows

Many of our customers in areas like Quorn, Rothley, and Woodhouse Eaves live in period properties with tall Victorian sash windows. Choosing the right shutter for these architectural gems is vital.

#### The Full Height Look

Full height shutters emphasise the grand proportions of Victorian windows. They create long, vertical lines that make a ceiling feel higher. If your goal is a minimalist, ‘uncluttered’ aesthetic, full height is the superior option. However, to access the window or let in direct light, you must either tilt the louvres or swing the large panels open, which requires clear floor space.

#### The Tier-on-Tier Look

Tier-on-tier shutters are often considered the ‘authentic’ choice for Victorian sash windows. They mirror the horizontal split of the sash frame itself. This style adds a level of architectural detail that feels quintessentially British and traditional. They are ideal for terrace houses where the window faces directly onto a busy pavement, as they solve the privacy issue without making the room feel dark.
