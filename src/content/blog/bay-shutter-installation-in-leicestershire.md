**Bay window shutters** are made to each section of the bay, rather than stretched across the opening on one rail. That is why they sit flush on splay, box and wider living-room bays across Leicestershire: every leaf follows the glass, so you get privacy, light control and a tidier street elevation.

These recent living-room installs show the same idea in different homes — white [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) fitted to five- and six-section bays.

## Why bays suit made-to-measure shutters

A bay is several windows meeting at an angle. Loose curtains or one wide blind tend to fight that geometry. Made-to-measure panels:

- **Follow each facet** of the bay
- **Keep privacy** at sofa height when louvres are closed
- **Hold daylight** when the slats are tilted open
- **Add a fitted look** that reads from the pavement as well as from the sofa

For typical fitted ranges, see the [bay window shutters cost guide](/bay-window-shutters-cost-uk-2026-guide/). Layout options — full-height, [tier-on-tier](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/) or [café-style](/cafe-style-shutters/) — depend on how overlooked the room is.
