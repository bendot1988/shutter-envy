A vertical blind is the made-to-measure option we specify when a **wide window or patio door** needs fabric louvres that stack to the side, not a single roller drop. Rotate the louvres for light and privacy. Draw them aside when you want the glass clear. That is the whole idea.

This guide is for homeowners in [Leicester](/locations/shutters-in-leicester/), [Loughborough](/locations/shutters-in-loughborough/) and Leicestershire. It covers how verticals work, where they earn their place, what they cost in 2026, and what we check before anything is ordered.

## The Short Answer

- **Vertical blinds suit patio doors, wide windows and long runs of glass.**
- **Louvres rotate** for light and privacy, then **stack to the side** when you want the opening clear.
- **A quality headrail matters.** We use the Vogue headrail system, available in colours to match fabric or décor.
- **They are not automatic blackout.** Fabric choice helps; edge light and gaps between louvres remain.
- **Plan on about £110–£180 fitted** for a standard window; wide patio doors cost more. See the [blinds cost guide](/how-much-do-made-to-measure-blinds-cost-uk-2026/).
- **A front bay still looks better in shutters.** Verticals are the fabric choice when width is the problem.
- **We measure on site.** Fabric, headrail colour and stack direction are chosen for that opening.

## What a Vertical Blind Is

[Our blinds range](/our-blinds/) includes roller, Venetian, honeycomb, duo roller and vertical. A **vertical blind** hangs fabric louvres from a headrail. You rotate them open or closed, then draw the stack left, right or split so the glass is clear.

That sideways stack is why verticals still earn a place on patio doors and wide openings. A single roller across a long span needs a heavy tube and can bow. Vertical louvres share the width and park neatly beside the frame when you want the doors free.

## Headrails, Fabrics and How They Operate

| Choice | What it means | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Vogue headrail | The track system we specify | Smooth travel; colours to match fabric or décor |
| Fabric louvres | Plain, textured or patterned | Look, wipe-clean feel and light control |
| Stack side | Left, right or split | Clears the door you use most |
| Rotate then draw | Tilt for privacy; pull aside for access | Daytime control without raising a full drop |

Fabric does more of the work than people expect. A wipe-clean louvre suits a busy family room. A softer weave suits a quieter lounge. Blackout or dim-out fabric helps in a darker room, but light can still show between louvres — that is normal for a vertical system.

![Close-up of vertical blind fabric louvres hanging from a white headrail, louvres evenly spaced](/images/blog/vertical-blinds-louvre-close-up.jpg)

Up close, the job is simple: even louvres, a level headrail, and a stack that clears the handle. Uneven gaps or a sticky track are what a proper survey and fit avoid.

## Where Vertical Blinds Earn Their Place

- **Patio doors**, when you want fabric control without a shutter or BlindScreen® budget on every opening.
- **Wide living-room or dining windows**, where a single roller would be too long.
- **Conservatory or garden-room runs of glass**, when shutters are not the brief (compare our [conservatory shutters guide](/conservatory-shutters-uk-buyers-guide/) if panels are still an option).
- **Offices and studies**, where rotating louvres cut glare on a screen.
- **A mid-budget wide opening** that needs a made-to-measure fit, not a cut-down off-the-shelf track.

Bifold doors are a different job. The blind has to clear the doors when they fold. Start with our [motorised blinds for bifold doors](/motorised-blinds-for-bifold-doors-uk-buyers-guide/) or [BlindScreen®](/blindscreen/) rather than treating a patio vertical as the same product.

## What It Costs in 2026

For a standard window, a made-to-measure vertical blind commonly sits around **£110–£180 fitted**. That is the planning range in our [blinds cost guide](/how-much-do-made-to-measure-blinds-cost-uk-2026/), which also prices rollers, Venetians, honeycomb, duo rollers and motorised systems.

Three things move a vertical above that band:

- **A wide patio or multi-pane opening**, because of the extra fabric and track length
- **Premium fabrics** and a contrasting headrail colour
- **Motorisation**, where the opening and usage justify it — see [blind motorisation](/blind-motorisation/)

We quote after measuring. Two patio doors that look the same from the garden often differ once the stack side, the handle clearance and the fabric weight are on the survey sheet.
