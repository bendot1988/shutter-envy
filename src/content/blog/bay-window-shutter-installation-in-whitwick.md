We recently completed a **bay window shutter installation in Whitwick** — Silk White [plantation shutters](/our-shutters/) across a five-section bay, specified with **76mm louvres**, **silent tilt** and **split tilt**.

## The spec

Bays like this need every panel made to follow the angle of its own section, so the shutters sit neatly in the reveal rather than fighting the shape of the window. **Silent tilt** removes the visible rod from the face of each panel, and **split tilt** lets the upper and lower louvres move independently — privacy below, daylight above.

**76mm louvres** keep the view open when tilted without looking oversized on a bay of this width. Our [louvre size guide](/shutter-louvre-sizes-uk-guide/) explains how we choose between sizes.
