We recently completed a **Boston shutter installation in Radcliffe on Trent**, Nottinghamshire — **Pure White** [Boston](/our-shutters/) shutters with **76mm louvres**, **split and hidden tilt**, and **matching hinges**.

## The spec

Boston is our hardwood-panel range — hardwood panels on an MDF frame — and it is a popular choice where homeowners want a crisp painted finish with a lighter panel. **Hidden tilt** removes the visible rod, **split tilt** lets the upper and lower louvres move independently, and **matching hinges** in the panel colour keep the whole window looking seamless.

**76mm louvres** open up the garden view when tilted. Our [louvre size guide](/shutter-louvre-sizes-uk-guide/) explains how we choose between sizes.
