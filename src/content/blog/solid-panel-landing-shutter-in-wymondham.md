## Why solid panel?

Not every shutter has louvres. [Solid panel shutters](/solid-panel-shutters-uk-buyers-guide/) are timber panels that open and close like small internal doors. When they are shut, they give a far more complete cover over the glass than a louvred shutter — useful for privacy, for darker rooms at night, and for a traditional look.

**Crisp Linen** is a soft off-white that sits comfortably against plain painted walls, so the shutter reads as part of the joinery rather than a window covering.
