This guide is written for Leicester homeowners who are actively considering buying shutters. We’ll cover the biggest price factors (by window type), realistic timelines from survey to installation, and the exact items you should expect to see on a professional quote in 2026.

### Why shutter costs vary so much

Shutters are almost always [made to measure](/why-made-to-measure-shutters-make-all-the-difference/). That’s the reason they look so good—clean lines, tidy frames, a premium finish—but it also means pricing changes based on your windows and your choices.

In Leicester, it’s common for neighbouring homes to pay very different amounts for shutters, simply because:

- One home has standard uPVC windows.

- Another has bays and awkward reveals.

- Another has a new-build with shallow frames and large patio openings.

- Another needs moisture-friendly options in bathrooms and kitchens.

### The biggest price factors for shutters in Leicester (2026)

**1. Window size**

Bigger windows mean more material and often more labour. Even within Leicester homes, you’ll see big variation between small bathroom windows, standard bedrooms, and large front-room openings or patio doors.

**2. Window shape and complexity**

This is where pricing can change quickly. Specialised shapes require more measuring precision and custom manufacturing:

- Bay windows (more angles and sections).

- [Arched window shutters](/bespoke-arched-window-shutters-shutter-envy-leicestershire/) (special shaping and layout).

- [Non-standard windows](/my-windows-arent-standard-size-can-i-still-get-shutters/) with unusual reveals.

![Checklist for choosing high quality shutters](/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/02-shutter-envy.jpeg)

**3. Material choice**

Different materials suit different rooms. Bathrooms and kitchens need options that cope better with humidity and temperature changes. You may need to compare [wood vs faux wood shutters](/the-difference-between-wooden-and-faux-wood-shutters/) to find the best fit for your budget and environment, particularly when selecting [bathroom shutters](/can-your-shutters-survive-the-bathroom/).

**4. Style and layout**

The style you choose affects both cost and everyday usability. Common choices include:

- Full height: Clean, simple, and great for privacy.

- Tier-on-tier: Top and bottom operate separately. Compare [full height vs tier-on-tier](/choosing-the-perfect-style-full-height-vs-tier-on-tier-shutters/) for flexibility.

- Café style: Lower half covered only. Learn more about [café style shutters](/what-are-cafe-style-shutters-and-what-is-the-point-of-them/) for light and privacy balance.

### Timeline: From survey to installation

A realistic shutter timeline in 2026 usually follows these steps:

- 1. Measure and design: A proper [survey to installation](/the-shutter-envy-process-from-survey-to-installation/) process starts with exact measurements and frame suitability checks.

- 2. Quote finalisation: Receiving a clear, itemised specification.

- 3. Manufacturing: The lead time while your bespoke shutters are built.

- 4. Installation day: Professional fitting, alignment, and operation demonstration.

### What your shutter quote should include

Use this checklist to ensure you are comparing like-for-like quotes:

- Product clarity: Exact rooms, shutter style, material, and colour.

- Fitting details: Confirmation of made-to-measure and specific frame types.

- Lead times: Estimated manufacturing and installation schedules.

- Aftercare: Warranty information and maintenance advice.

- Payment terms: Total cost (including VAT), deposit, and payment schedule.
